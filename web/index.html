FAQ

Common questions.

More detail — including disputes and account help — lives in the in-app Support Center.

A brand locks USDC into the InfluenceEscrow smart contract when hiring a creator. The creator delivers and submits proof; the brand approves and the contract releases 99% to the creator and 1% to Influence — automatically, on-chain, with no manual payout step on either side.
48 hours after proof is submitted, anyone can trigger an automatic release to the creator. A brand going silent can't hold funds hostage indefinitely.
Influence runs on Arc Testnet right now — Circle's public test network for Arc, using testnet USDC with no real-world value. Every transaction is genuinely on-chain and verifiable, funded via the public Circle faucet rather than real deposits.
Listing a profile is self-serve and open to anyone. The "✓ Verified" badge is granted by the Influence team after manually confirming a creator's identity and social presence — request it from the in-app Support Center.
1% of the deal, deducted automatically when funds release — enforced by the contract itself (FEE_BPS = 100), not a policy that can quietly change. No listing fees, no subscription.
